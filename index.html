    How progress works

    Finishing a quiz counts as a day. Do one on consecutive days and the streak grows; miss a day and it starts again from one. Each quiz pays XP once a day, so coming back tomorrow for a review pays again, and lessons come due for review after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.

    Ten minutes a session. Pick the lesson marked "Review due" first; if none is due, take the next new one.
